# VC/Funding Cluster: Concept Overlap Evaluation

## Executive Summary

This evaluation analyzes concept overlap across 5 VC/funding reports from 3 geographies, all within the same domain. The goal is to measure how consistently venture capital metrics are defined and reported across different sources and countries.

**Reports Evaluated:**

**Total variables extracted:** 465 across 5 reports.

## Definition and Measurement Comparison

For concepts appearing in 2+ reports, key differences include:

### Deal Volume / Deal Count
- **SG 2022:** Equity funding rounds only, excludes debt/bridge/ICOs/grants
- **SE US 2025:** All VC deals, broader definition
- **SG 2024:** VC deals involving Singapore-HQ firms (PitchBook + EnterpriseSG data)
- **UK 2024/2025:** Equity investment into small businesses (Beauhurst data), includes business angels
- **Classification:** Same concept, different scope (equity-only vs all VC vs small business equity)

### Total Deal Value / Investment Value
- **SG 2022:** Equity funding rounds in USD, Singapore/ASEAN focus
- **SE US 2025:** All VC capital in B USD, Southeast US
- **SG 2024:** VC deals involving SG-HQ firms in USD billions
- **UK 2024/2025:** Equity investment in GBP billions, UK small businesses
- **Classification:** Same concept, different currency (USD vs GBP), different scope

### Average Deal Size
- **SG 2022:** Stage-specific medians (Seed/A/B) in USD
- **SE US 2025:** Overall average check size in M USD
- **UK 2024/2025:** Average deal size in GBP, includes business angels
- **Classification:** Same concept, different measurement (median vs average), different currency

### Deal Distribution by Stage
- **SG 2022:** Deal size ranges in USD
- **SG 2024:** Early/late stage classification
- **UK 2024/2025:** Seed/Venture/Growth lifecycle stages
- **Classification:** Similar concept, different categorization schemes

### International Comparison
- **SG 2022:** ASEAN-6 market distribution
- **SG 2024:** Global top-10 ecosystem comparison
- **UK 2024/2025:** UK vs US VC investment gaps, GDP-adjusted comparisons
- **Classification:** Same concept, different comparison frameworks

## Implications for Innovation/Startup/Entrepreneurship Indicators

### 1. VC Metrics Are Becoming Standardized
The universal presence of Deal Volume and Deal Value across all 5 reports suggests these are effectively "standard" VC metrics. Any cross-source comparison framework should start with these two.

### 2. Definition Divergence Remains a Barrier
Even within the same report series (UK Equity Tracker 2024 → 2025), definitions drift. Cross-geography comparisons require careful harmonization of:
- Currency (USD vs GBP)
- Scope (equity-only vs all VC vs small business equity)
- Measurement (median vs average)
- Data source (PitchBook vs Beauhurst vs EnterpriseSG)

### 3. UK Reports Are the Most Comprehensive
The UK Equity Tracker series covers 15 concepts including angel investment, spinout activity, and sector-specific metrics. This makes it the richest source for VC indicator development.

### 4. Singapore Reports Show Domain Expansion
SG Venture 2024 introduced ecosystem size indicators and RIE domain breakdowns that weren't in the 2022 version, suggesting Singapore is expanding its VC measurement framework.

### 5. The SE US Report Is Narrow but Focused
With only 5 variables, the SE US report is the most focused. It covers the core VC metrics but lacks the depth of the UK or SG reports.

## Recommendations

1. **Anchor on Deal Volume and Deal Value** as universal VC metrics — they appear everywhere
2. **Standardize currency reporting** — all reports should provide USD equivalents alongside local currency
3. **Adopt the UK Equity Tracker's stage taxonomy** (Seed/Venture/Growth) as a cross-geography standard
4. **Clarify scope boundaries** — each report should explicitly state whether it covers equity-only, all VC, or includes business angels
5. **Harmonize measurement methods** — standardize on either median or average deal size (or report both)
